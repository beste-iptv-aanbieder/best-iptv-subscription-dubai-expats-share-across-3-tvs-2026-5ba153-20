Why stability matters for three TVs at once

When you share a subscription across three TVs, each stream uses bandwidth. A weak server will buffer or freeze. SubTV IP runs an anti-freeze server cluster that handles multiple connections without lag. That 99.9% uptime means you rarely see a spinning wheel.

Generic budget resellers often share one overloaded server among hundreds of users. During prime time – for example, Friday evenings in Dubai – streams stall or drop. That is the worst time to watch with family.

Expat server lines try to cover many countries, but the long distance between servers causes audio to fall out of sync. On a third TV the problem gets worse because the network has to split resources.

VOD-only providers store movies on fast servers, but their live streams come from cheap sources. If you need live content on all three TVs, you will face frequent buffering.
